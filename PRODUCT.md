# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Investors and traders monitoring multi-market equity positions across US exchanges and Thai SET (.BK). Needs rapid scanning of live price movements, 24h change percentages, and feed freshness.

## Product Purpose
Jod-Hoon (จดหุ้น) provides real-time portfolio watchlist tracking spanning US and Thai equities, offering continuous data synchronization, market-filtered views, and responsive live status.

## Positioning
Unified dual-market equity watchlist combining international tech giants with Thai blue-chip equities in an executive-tier, luxury fintech interface.

## Operating Context
Daily trading hours, multi-monitor setups, mobile check-ins. High-frequency visual reference where numerical precision, status certainty, and low visual fatigue are paramount.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React 19 + Vite frontend in plain JavaScript JSX (no TypeScript, no Tailwind).
- Go HTTP backend with PostgreSQL and Yahoo Finance feed.
- Features: 60s auto-refresh countdown, instant refresh button, system health status, market tabs (All / US / SET), live ticker/company search.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Jod-Hoon (จดหุ้น) - Multi-Market Portfolio & Live Watchlist
- Visual language: Luxury Fintech Hub. Deep obsidian dark base (`#07050a` / `#0d0914`), radiant metallic gold accents (`#eab308`, `#fbbf24`, `#f59e0b` with radial glows), cut with rich electric violet and deep amethyst tones (`#7c3aed`, `#8b5cf6`, `#1a0f2e`).
- Voice: Modern, crisp, confident, professional.

## Product Principles
1. High-velocity clarity: Prices, 24h shifts, and market tags stand out with zero ambiguity.
2. Balanced luminescence: Glowing gold accentuates focal points; violet tones ground and soften background layers.
3. Clean responsiveness: Flawless layout on desktop widescreen down to mobile handheld.
